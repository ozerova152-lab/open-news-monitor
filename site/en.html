Open News Monitor

Follow the sites you choose and get new publications with links to the originals.

GitHubHow to install

What it does

Collects new items from selected public sources, applies your filters and keeps the original links.

Who it helps

  • Social media managers and news channel admins can track developments worth covering and gather links to original sources for their posts.
  • Newsletter writers and small editorial teams can follow their topics and find material for roundups.
  • Anyone who regularly checks several websites can get new items from selected sources in one digest.

How it works

  1. Choose sources
  2. Verify publications
  3. SQLite remembers known links
  4. The program builds a digest
  5. Enabled channels receive it

Which sites can you follow?

Give your assistant links to sites you already read or tell it what topic you want to follow. For example, ask for sources on apartment prices in your city. The assistant will suggest sites with links and check which ones can be connected. You choose which to follow.

Some sites need extra setup. If you have a long list of links, you can keep it in a spreadsheet. Source list guide (in Russian)

Delivery channels

  • Markdown — a digest on your computer
  • Telegram — messages through your bot
  • Email — a digest via SMTP, including Gmail
  • Google Sheets — rows in your spreadsheet

When does Open News Monitor check sites?

Start by running Open News Monitor whenever you want a fresh digest. For scheduled runs, ask your assistant to set up and check automatic runs on your powered-on computer or on a separate always-on Linux server. Windows may block this setup on some computers.

FAQ

Can I run the monitor on this page?

This is a showcase. Install the program on your computer and run it from a terminal.

How do I avoid sending old news?

Start with safe initialization. It remembers current publications without delivering them.

Are descriptions translated?

Telegram and email can show a shortened source description. There is no built-in AI translation.

What has been checked?

Telegram and Gmail passed live delivery tests. Google Sheets passed a prior live test, with no fresh access check. Excel 2010 imported the CSV, but saving CSV UTF-8 remains unverified.
